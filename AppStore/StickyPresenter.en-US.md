## Description (4000자)

While you present, the two things you need most are the hardest to see: your notes and the clock. StickyPresenter keeps both floating above whatever is on screen — including full-screen Keynote — so you never have to leave your slides to check either one.

It lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon and nothing running in a window you have to manage.

FLOATING STICKY NOTES
• Stay on top of full-screen presentations, where a normal window cannot follow
• Six colors, adjustable opacity and font size
• Lock a note in place so a stray click cannot move it
• Create one instantly from whatever is on your clipboard
• Show or hide every note at once when you want a clean screen

COUNTDOWN TIMERS
• Square widgets you can resize until they read from the back of the room
• Light and dark themes, so the timer sits on your slides rather than fighting them
• A chime and a pulsing border when time is up
• Open any timer as its own window and add it as a source in screen sharing or AirPlay
• Hide a timer from the audience, or pull a lost one back next to the panel
• Pomodoro mode alternates focus and break for as long as you are working

TELEPROMPTER
• Auto-scrolling script with adjustable speed and font size
• Mirror mode for a teleprompter glass or a reflected setup

NOTIFICATION CENTER WIDGET
• The running timer keeps counting down in Notification Center, so the time is one swipe away

BACKGROUND MUSIC
• Four moods — Focus, Calm, Jazz, Upbeat — for rehearsing or for the room before you start
• Music can start and stop with the timer, fading in and out
• You supply the audio: the app creates a folder for each mood on first launch and plays whatever you drop in, so nothing is licensed on your behalf

IPHONE REMOTE (SEPARATE FREE APP)
During a talk your Mac is out of reach. Remote Controller puts the timer in your hand — start and pause, add or subtract 30 seconds, resize the widget, move it off a slide it is covering, or start a preset timer. It pairs with your Mac over your local network with a four-digit code, so in a room full of Macs it only ever talks to yours.

PRIVACY
StickyPresenter collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no internet requests at all. The only networking it does is the direct, encrypted connection to the iPhone remote on your own local network. Your notes and scripts never leave your Mac.

Requires macOS 14 or later.

## What's New — 1.0.9 (4000자)

Timer windows can be dragged again on macOS 27, and the close button now sits where it does on every other Mac window.

MACOS 27
• Fixed timer widgets and timer windows that would not move when dragged on macOS 27. The spot you grab stays under the cursor.
• Works on the first click, even while another app is in front.

CLOSE BUTTON
• Moved to the top-left, as the same red button macOS uses to close a window. Hover over the timer to see it, and over the button to see the ×.
