Your script and your clock, always in view

StickyPresenter is a lightweight macOS menu-bar app that keeps sticky notes, countdown timers, and a teleprompter floating above your full-screen Keynote or any presentation — so your notes and timing stay visible while you present.

  • Floating sticky notes — stay on top of full-screen slides, in six colors, with adjustable opacity and font size, and a lock so nothing changes by accident.
  • Timers — resizable countdown widgets with a chime and a pulsing border when time is up. Open one in its own window to share it over screen sharing or AirPlay.
  • Teleprompter — an auto-scrolling script with adjustable speed and font size, plus a mirror mode.
  • Lives in the menu bar — no Dock icon and no extra windows to keep track of.

Companion app

Remote Controller for iPhone

During a talk your Mac is out of reach. Remote Controller puts the timer in your hand — start and pause, add 30 seconds, resize the widget, and drag it somewhere else on your Mac's screen when it covers a slide. It connects over your local network, with no account and nothing to set up.
